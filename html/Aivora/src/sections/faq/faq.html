Questions, answered

Everything you need to know about plans, billing and rollout. If you cannot find what you are looking for, our team is one message away.

01 How does the 14-day trial work?

Every workspace starts on the full Pro tier for 14 days, no card required. You keep access to all seats and integrations, and we send a reminder three days before it ends so nothing lapses unexpectedly.

02 Can I change plans or seats later?

Absolutely. Upgrades apply instantly and are prorated to the day, while downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. You can add or remove seats at any time from the workspace settings panel.

03 Is my data secure and portable?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we are SOC 2 Type II certified. You own your content outright and can export a full JSON or CSV archive whenever you like, with no lock-in.

04 Do you offer discounts for teams?

Teams over 25 seats qualify for volume pricing, and we extend a 40% discount to registered non-profits and educational institutions. Reach out and we will tailor a quote within one business day.

05 What does support look like?

Every plan includes email support with a median first response under four hours. Pro and Enterprise workspaces also get priority chat and a dedicated onboarding specialist to help with migration.
